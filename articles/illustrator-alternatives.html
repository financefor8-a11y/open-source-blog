2 Best Free Open-Source Alternatives to Adobe Illustrator

Open Source Pro
Published Oct 26, 2026 • 4 min read

Vector graphics shouldn't cost a monthly fee. These free alternatives rival Illustrator in almost every way.

Advertisement

1. Inkscape

The industry standard open-source vector graphics editor. It has everything you need for logos and illustrations.

  • Cost: 100% Free
  • Privacy: No forced cloud saves

2. Vectornator (Linearity)

A highly intuitive vector design tool that feels incredibly modern, fast, and works seamlessly on Apple devices.

  • Cost: Free
  • Privacy: Local saving allowed
