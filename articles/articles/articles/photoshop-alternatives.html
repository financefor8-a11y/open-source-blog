4 Best Free & Open-Source Alternatives to Adobe Photoshop (2026)

If you are a freelance designer or hobbyist, Adobe's monthly subscription fees can quickly eat into your budget. The open-source community has built incredibly powerful, completely free alternatives that rival Photoshop's core features.

1. GIMP (GNU Image Manipulation Program)

When people ask for a free Photoshop alternative, GIMP is almost always the first answer. It has been in development for over two decades and packs a massive suite of tools.

  • Pros: Features advanced layer masks, bezier curves, filters, and extensive plugin support.
  • Cons: The learning curve can be steep if you are used to Adobe's shortcuts.
  • Platform: Windows, macOS, Linux

2. Krita

While GIMP focuses heavily on photo manipulation, Krita was built from the ground up for digital artists. If you primarily use a drawing tablet, Krita is actually preferred by many professionals over Adobe.

  • Pros: Incredible brush engine, brush stabilizers, and great animation tools.
  • Cons: Not optimized for standard photo-retouching.

3. Photopea

Photopea is a full-fledged photo editor that runs entirely in your web browser. You don't need to download or install anything. The interface is an almost exact clone of Photoshop.

  • Pros: Opens and exports native .PSD files flawlessly. Runs on any device.
  • Cons: Contains sidebar advertisements, requires internet connection.
