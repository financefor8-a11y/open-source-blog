2 Best Free Open-Source Alternatives to Adobe Premiere Pro

Open Source Pro Published Oct 25, 2026 • 5 min read

Video editing software can be incredibly expensive. If you want to start a YouTube channel or edit professional videos without paying Adobe's hefty monthly fees, the open-source community has some phenomenal free options.

Advertisement

1. Kdenlive

Kdenlive is one of the most popular open-source video editors. It is highly versatile and supports almost all audio and video formats without needing to convert or re-encode your clips.

  • Pros: Multi-track video editing, automatic backup, great community support.
  • Cons: Can occasionally be buggy on older Windows computers.

2. Shotcut

Shotcut is a sleek, cross-platform video editor that doesn't require any import processes. You can drag your raw 4K footage right onto the timeline and start cutting immediately.

  • Pros: Native timeline editing, massive library of video filters, supports 4K resolutions.
  • Cons: The interface takes a little time to get used to if you are coming from Premiere.
